How the value is scored

Every implied value is this entry’s own claim, computed from the snapshot’s own figures only — never from a list of names:

implied value = TVL × 0.35 × momentum × age × category

  • TVL × 0.35 — at launch, a protocol token’s fully diluted value commonly lands at a fraction of TVL; 0.35 is the central case.
  • Momentum = 1 + 2 × 7-day TVL change, clamped to ±30% (0.40–1.60). Growing TVL is worth more; shrinking TVL is worth less.
  • Age = 1 + 0.25 × years listed with no token, capped at 4 years (1.00–2.00). Surviving longer without a token is pent-up launch value.
  • Category = 1.15 for fee-earning venues (DEX, lending, derivatives), 1.10 for yield, RWA, curators and allocators, 1.05 for CDPs and basis, 1.00 otherwise.

Protocols that record no token are the board’s subject. Protocols whose token already trades are ranked below them, with their real market cap shown. Exchanges, bridges and wrapped or staked products cannot launch a token of their own, so they sit at the bottom with a nominal marker (0.01% of TVL) instead of a launch valuation. A protocol is always described as recording no token, never as having none.
